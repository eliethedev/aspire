# Key Features & Functions

## 1. AI-Powered COT Module
**Function:** Real-time digital encoding of observation ratings with immediate Machine Learning analysis.

**Impact:** Instead of raw numbers, the system provides an instant breakdown of a teacher's strengths and weaknesses per PPST indicator.

---

## 2. The "SuperviseBot" AI Chat Assistant
**Function:** A conversational AI that guides supervisors in interpreting COT results and drafting the Enhanced Post-Observation Conference Form.

**Impact:** Enhances the quality of feedback by suggesting professional coaching language and research-based strategies tailored to the observation ratings.

---

## 3. Predictive Analytics Dashboard
**Function:** A data visualization hub that uses Predictive Analytics to track "Growth Velocity" across schools and districts.

**Impact:** Enables School Heads and Division Supervisors to move from reactive management to proactive instructional leadership.

---

## 4. Integrated Notification & Alert Hub
**Function:** An automated communication system that sends real-time updates to all users via multiple channels (in-app, SMS, or Email).

**Impact:**
- **School Heads:** Get alerts when supervision cycles are completed or when a teacher's ratings indicate a need for urgent support.
- **Supervisors:** Receive reminders for pending post-observation conferences and tracking of agreed-upon "Next Steps."

---

## 5. Dynamic & Scalable Architecture
**Function:** A modular system design that supports the continuous addition of new functionalities.

**Impact:** Ensures the system remains relevant long-term, capable of adopting new evaluation rubrics or reporting requirements without a total overhaul.

---

## Automated User Notification Flow

| Recipient | Trigger Event | Notification Content |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|
| Teacher | Pre-Observation Scheduled | Date, time, and link to upload digital lesson materials to the Cloud. |
| Supervisor | COT Ratings Encoded | AI Assistant prompt: "Observation complete. Click here to draft the Enhanced Post-Obs Form." |
| School Head | Form Signed/Finalized | Summary of the COT results and the "Agreements" for school-level tracking. |
| All Users | Pending Requirements | Automated reminder of upcoming deadlines to ensure the 3-stage cycle is completed on time. |
